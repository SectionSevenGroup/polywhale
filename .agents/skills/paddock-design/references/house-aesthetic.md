# Product identity and source authority

Begin with the product's current approved design profile, assets, and running interface. If the repo has a design manifest, use it to locate the adopted revision. Treat historical screenshots and mockups as evidence for their own revision.

Use the product's typography, semantic colours, mark, spacing roles, control families, and interaction patterns. Keep hierarchy, alignment, feedback, and menu behaviour consistent within the product. Product-specific colours, type and motion stay with that product. For a new product without an approved profile, identify any provisional decisions in the handoff.

Use shared design guidance to review usability and craft. Bring changes to common components back to their owning implementation. Product profiles retain the specific visual and interaction decisions for each app.
