# Dieter Rams principles and philosophy

Version 1.1.0 · 12 November 2026 · Portfolio companion reference

Use design to help people accomplish something worthwhile with clarity, care and restraint. Reduce unnecessary demands on their attention while making the remaining experience work well. The objective is useful quality over time, rather than an empty screen or a recognisable designer's look.

## Rams' ten ideas

Use the ten principles in Rams' original order. The short labels use Rams' wording and the last column applies the ideas to our work. Read the complete original text at the [Rams Foundation, 2002 version](https://rams-foundation.org/foundation/design-comprehension/theses/) or [Vitsœ](https://www.vitsoe.com/us/about/good-design).

| ID | Rams' wording | Application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R01 | Innovative | Advance the product through useful invention. |
| R02 | Useful | Make the object serve its intended purpose. |
| R03 | Aesthetic | Treat visual quality as part of everyday usefulness. |
| R04 | Understandable | Make structure and operation readily intelligible. |
| R05 | Unobtrusive | Leave space for the person and their work. |
| R06 | Honest | Represent capabilities and value truthfully. |
| R07 | Long-lasting | Design for enduring use through platform changes and maintenance. |
| R08 | Thorough down to the last detail | Resolve even the smallest details with care. |
| R09 | Environmentally friendly | Consider bandwidth, battery, storage, repairability and lifecycle waste. |
| R10 | As little design as possible | Retain essentials and remove unnecessary additions. |

The Foundation introduces these ideas as evolving considerations, rather than immutable commandments. Their application requires judgement as technology, culture and people's needs change. [Source: Rams Foundation](https://rams-foundation.org/foundation/design-comprehension/theses/).

## The broader philosophy

Rams' thinking connects everyday usefulness with responsibility for the things designers bring into the world. His work for Braun and Vitsœ links products into considered systems. The furniture work illustrates continuity and development over time; his 1976 speech addresses finite resources and waste. These concerns go beyond a restrained visual style. [Source: Vitsœ on Rams](https://www.vitsoe.com/us/about/dieter-rams).

## Applying the philosophy to our products

Everything below is our contemporary interpretation for software and services, not a statement Rams made about apps, accessibility or AI.

### R01 — Innovation with a reason

Start with the obstacle a person faces. Introduce an unfamiliar pattern, technology or AI step only when it gives a demonstrable benefit over a familiar approach. Novelty alone does not justify cost, latency or learning. Compare the proposed journey with the existing one before choosing.

### R02 — A complete useful task

Let someone finish the core job, including correction and recovery. A feature is incomplete if it looks convincing but cannot save, restore, export or perform its advertised action. Preserve manual work where AI or network failure would otherwise block the product's purpose. Use real task outcomes to judge usefulness.

### R03 — Visual care that supports use

Use an intentional hierarchy, coherent typography and measured relationships between content and controls. Inspect actual rendered text, image crops, optical alignment and light/dark states. Beauty may include warmth, colour or expressive motion when appropriate to the approved product. Do not make different products look identical to prove consistency.

### R04 — Comprehensible operation

Make the current context, available action and resulting state understandable. Name actions with familiar language. Distinguish Back, Cancel, Close, Save and Delete. Use progressive disclosure for secondary complexity while keeping the main path findable. An interface that needs a tutorial for every ordinary task needs revision.

### R05 — Restraint and user control

Keep attention on the person's activity. Make notifications, sound, motion and assistant speech purposeful and controllable. Keep interruptions proportionate and preserve stop, mute and dismissal. Calmness can coexist with sufficient contrast, readable text and a prominent primary action. Hiding useful controls is not restraint.

### R06 — Honest capability and state

Show what actually happened: drafted, saved locally, queued, synced, submitted or delivered. Do not simulate progress or successful completion. Present AI uncertainty when it affects a decision, and distinguish a proposal from an executed action. Do not imply certification, professional approval or a working integration without evidence.

### R07 — Continuity and maintainability

Keep familiar conventions, stable information architecture and durable records. Improve shared primitives instead of accumulating screen-specific patches. Preserve migrations, export and recovery where applicable. Change a convention for a better outcome, not merely to keep up with a visual trend. Longevity permits thoughtful evolution.

### R08 — Finish the small things

Check spacing roles, baselines, line wrapping, truncation, icon weight, target bounds, focus and pressed states. Reconcile the same component across screens. Inspect empty, long, loading, offline, denied and failed states. Treat keyboard, screen-reader and enlarged-text use as part of the product, not optional polish. Use adopted platform values; never invent a Rams spacing system.

### R09 — Responsible resource use

Avoid unnecessary background listening, polling, network transfers, duplicate storage, oversized media and model calls. Measure battery, data, storage or inference cost where material. Idle effects and voice sessions should respect product budgets and device state. Do not infer a quantified environmental benefit without measurements and a defensible method.

### R10 — Remove what does not earn its place

Give each element a purpose: action, orientation, explanation, feedback, necessary content or approved expression. Consolidate duplication and avoid competing primary actions. Remove an element only after checking that its role is preserved. A cleaner screenshot is not progress if someone must guess, tap more or lose access to a task.

## Boundaries

- Preserve the product's approved personality and the information hierarchy that serves its users.
- Retain approved accent roles, evidence context, and any controls users need for real work.
- Use the adopted Apple and portfolio standards for technical requirements, accessibility, devices and integration. This companion introduces no new universal font, grid, radius or gesture.
- Apply relevant principles to every task; do not run a ten-part visual audit for a backend-only correction.
- Resolve real conflicts against current owner direction and governing project requirements. Record a trade-off rather than declaring an aesthetic preference objectively correct.
